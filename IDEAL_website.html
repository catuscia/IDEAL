The project

Principled approaches to ethical AI

IDEAL is an active Inria Associate Team for 2025–2027, connecting research communities in France, Australia and Brazil.

Artificial intelligence increasingly supports decisions that affect people's lives. But the data and models behind those decisions can reveal sensitive information or reproduce biases that lead to unfair outcomes.

IDEAL studies privacy and fairness together, with a strong foundation in quantitative information flow (QIF), differential privacy, metric privacy and formal analysis. Our research seeks mathematical understanding alongside practical mechanisms that can be evaluated in real-world settings.

By combining complementary expertise in information theory, privacy, fairness, machine learning and natural language processing, the collaboration supports research exchange and the development of trustworthy AI technologies.

Our scientific priorities

  • Improve the balance between privacy protection and data utility.
  • Understand different mathematical and societal notions of fairness.
  • Characterise the relationships, tensions and possible synergies between privacy and fairness.

Scientific programme

Three connected research objectives

IDEAL combines rigorous theory with applications in data anonymisation, learning systems and language technologies.

Objective 01

Improving the privacy–utility trade-off

We investigate metric-privacy mechanisms that protect sensitive information while preserving usefulness, including information-theoretic approaches based on the Blahut–Arimoto algorithm.

The programme considers anonymisation of microdata, privacy protection for large language models, and privacy-preserving machine and federated learning.

Metric privacyMicrodataLLMs
Objective 02

A unifying framework for fairness

There are multiple definitions of algorithmic fairness, based on different principles and sometimes incompatible assumptions. IDEAL explores how to model and compare these notions in a common mathematical language.

Quantitative information flow provides a foundation for their formal and quantitative analysis.

Fairness notionsQIFFormal analysis
Objective 03

Understanding privacy and fairness together

Privacy and fairness can reinforce one another in some contexts and conflict in others. We study their interaction using formal models that make assumptions and guarantees explicit.

The objective is a clearer understanding of the trade-offs and practical guidance for responsible AI.

Ethical AIPrivacy & fairnessResponsible AI
Our methodological approach

Information-theoretic foundations, mathematical models, and empirical assessment of privacy and utility — linking theoretical guarantees to applied problems in data publication and machine learning.
